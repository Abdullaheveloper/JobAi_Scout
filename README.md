# JobAI Scout

JobAI Scout is a career platform that helps people find jobs, keep their profile ready for applications, and (for hiring teams) manage posted roles and applicants. New accounts wait for approval before full access.

The product has three kinds of signed-in users:

- **Job seekers** — search and save jobs, manage a career profile and CV, schedule automated job searches, use voice career help, and fill application forms with a browser extension.
- **Recruiters** — maintain a company profile, post jobs, review applicants, and track hiring status.
- **Administrators** — approve accounts, manage users and jobs, and view platform activity.

Anyone can visit the public site (home, about, contact, privacy) and sign up or sign in without being logged in first.

---

## Getting started

1. Create an account as a **job seeker** or **recruiter**.
2. Wait until an administrator approves your account (you will see a waiting screen until then).
3. Sign in. You are taken to the area that matches your account type.

You can reset a forgotten password from the sign-in flow. Language and light/dark appearance can be changed in the product; your choice is remembered.

A cookie preference banner appears until you accept or reject optional cookies.

## Job seekers

After approval, job seekers use a personal workspace with these capabilities.

### Dashboard
See a greeting, skills and suggested roles from your profile, and shortcuts to the main tools. Some summary numbers on this screen are illustrative placeholders, not live counts.

### CV and career profile
- Upload a CV (PDF or Word) for analysis.
- Review extracted details and resume improvement suggestions.
- Edit personal information, skills, desired roles, salary and work preferences, social links, and photo.
- Build a structured career history (experience, education, projects, achievements, references).
- Set answers and safety preferences used when auto-filling application forms.

### Job discovery
- Search with keywords and location, and run a live job collection across supported sources.
- Browse matched recommendations and collected results.
- Save jobs, open external apply links, and generate a tailored cover letter to copy (the letter is not sent for you).
- Apply in-app when the listing was posted by a recruiter on this platform; otherwise Apply opens the external job page.

### Saved jobs
View and unsave jobs you bookmarked from the job board or from extension-related recommendations. Open their apply links when available.

### Applications
View applications you have submitted through the platform (status, job details, cover letter preview). This screen exists but is not linked in the main side menu—open it from a direct link if you know the path, or ask for it to be added to navigation.

### Automated job search
Create schedules (once, daily, weekly, specific days, or monthly) so job collection can run on a timetable using your profile’s desired role and location. Turn schedules on or off, or delete them.

### Form fill (browser extension)
The in-app Form Fill page explains how to install the **Job Form Fill** browser extension and what kinds of fields it can fill. The actual filling happens in the browser on supported job sites (for example Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Ashby, LinkedIn, Indeed, Google Forms, and similar). You sign in through the extension, load your profile, and fill forms with review for sensitive questions.

### Voice career assistant
Talk to a voice assistant: speak questions, get spoken answers grounded in career knowledge, review history, and optionally upload documents into that knowledge base. A separate conversational voice agent page also exists but is not listed in the side menu.

### Settings
Update profile, career history, autofill preferences, language, and appearance.

## Recruiters

After approval, recruiters use a hiring workspace.

### Company profile
Enter company name, website, industry, and description.

### Jobs
Create, edit, and delete job posts (title, company, location, type, salary, skills, requirements, apply URL). Open “post job” from the menu to start a new listing.

### Applicants
See people who applied to your jobs. Move them through statuses: new, shortlisted, rejected, or hired. Add private notes and view a short candidate profile (name, email, skills, whether a resume is on file).

### Application status
See counts and a list of applications by status for your jobs. This view is read-only; change status on the Applicants screen.

## Administrators

Administrators are not blocked by account approval. They can:

- See overview counts (users, pending approvals, jobs, applications) and extension usage summaries.
- Filter users by approval status; approve or reject pending accounts; promote or demote between admin and job-seeker roles; edit profile fields; permanently delete users (with confirmation).
- Manage job collection sources (RSS and company career feeds) and browse or delete jobs in the catalog.
- View platform analytics over selectable time ranges (signups, roles, job sources, locations, extension and voice activity).
- Configure and inspect voice / knowledge-base settings on a dedicated admin voice screen (reachable by direct URL; not shown in the main admin menu today).

Pending accounts that are not decided within the configured window expire; those users can renew their request when signing in again.

## Public pages

- **Home** — product introduction and links to register or sign in (including a recruiter path).
- **About** — company/product story and static marketing content.
- **Contact** — a message form that shows a success message in the browser; it does not send email through the product today.
- **Privacy** — privacy policy text and contact address for privacy questions.

---

## Languages and appearance

The interface is available in English, French, German, and Hindi. You can switch language and toggle light or dark theme; signed-in preferences are stored with your account when available.
